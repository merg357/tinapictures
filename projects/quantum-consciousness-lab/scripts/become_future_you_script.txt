Find a comfortable place where you can remain undisturbed for the next several minutes.

Sit upright if that is comfortable for you.

Let your hands rest naturally.

Close your eyes.

And for this time, allow yourself to stop doing.

There is nowhere else you need to be.

There is nothing you need to solve right now.

You do not need to think about what happened earlier.

You do not need to think about what happens next.

For this time, your only responsibility is to be here.

Right now.

Take a slow breath in through your nose.

And gently breathe out.

Again.

Slowly breathe in.

And breathe out.

One more time.

Breathe in deeply.

And allow the breath to leave your body slowly.

Begin to notice your face.

Relax your forehead.

Relax the muscles around your eyes.

Let your jaw loosen.

Allow your tongue to rest.

Relax your neck.

Let your shoulders drop.

Release any tension you have been carrying.

Relax your arms.

Your hands.

Your fingers.

Relax your chest.

Your stomach.

Your hips.

Your thighs.

Your knees.

Your lower legs.

Your ankles.

And your feet.

Let your body become still.

You do not have to force yourself to relax.

Simply give your body permission to stop holding on.

[PAUSE 15]

Now become aware of your breathing.

Do not try to control it.

Simply notice it.

Feel the breath enter your body.

Feel the breath leave your body.

Notice the small moment of stillness between each breath.

Breathe in.

And breathe out.

[PAUSE 10]

The space between your fingers.

The space around your palms.

The space around your wrists.

Rather than concentrating only on the physical body, begin noticing the space around the body.

[PAUSE 15]

Become aware of your entire body sitting here.

Imagine that your attention is becoming wider.

Less focused on one point.

Less focused on one thought.

Less focused on the physical world immediately around you.

Simply become aware.

For the next few moments, allow yourself to forget your normal identity.

You do not have to be your job right now.

You do not have to be your responsibilities.

You do not have to be your problems.

You do not have to be your schedule.

You do not have to be your past.

For this moment, allow all of those things to remain outside this meditation.

You are simply awareness.

Present.

Here.

Now.

[PAUSE 20]

Simply notice when your attention has wandered.

And gently return.

Return to your breath.

Return to the space around your body.

Return to this moment.

Every time you notice that your mind has wandered and you return your attention, you are practicing.

There is nothing to judge.

There is nothing to get wrong.

Come back.

Again and again.
